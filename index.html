THE STORY BEHIND THE STORIES

A world builder
with a love for stories.

Gabriella Michael is a Nigerian author and screenwriter passionate about fantasy, romance, film, and emotionally driven stories. She creates worlds that explore love, identity, destiny, power, heartbreak, mystery, and the extraordinary hidden beyond ordinary life.

She is the creator of multiple fictional worlds and the founder of Gabriella Michael Studios, where stories move from page to screen.
