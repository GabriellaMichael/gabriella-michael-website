01 / THE AUTHOR

She writes the worlds
she wants to live in.

Gabriella Michael is a Nigerian author and screenwriter passionate about fantasy, romance, film, and emotionally driven stories. She creates stories that explore love, identity, destiny, power, heartbreak, mystery, and the extraordinary worlds hidden beyond ordinary life.

She is the creator of multiple fictional worlds and the founder of Gabriella Michael Studios, where stories move from page to screen.
